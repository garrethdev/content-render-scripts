## Format rules (taken from the reference)

| Slot | Rule | Reference length |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** (slide 1) | `5 viral theories / that will / ACTUALLY / <outcome>`: 4 short lines, sentence case, one word in ALL CAPS | ~50 chars |
| **Hook sub** (slide 1) | `(from <skeptic persona>)`: 2 lines, in parentheses, smaller | ~30 chars |
| **Title** (slides 2–6) | `N. the <name> theory.`: all lowercase, ends in a period, one line | 24–30 chars |
| **Body para 1** | The setup: an everyday observation or a science fact. All lowercase | 80–140 chars |
| **Body para 2** | The reframe or payoff. Ends on a short punchy line ("trust the process." / "always was.") | 85–180 chars |
| **Product plug** | Theory #3 only. First person, casual, last sentence of para 2: "i like using ___ to ___." | 1 sentence |

Tone: lowercase, no emojis, no exclamation marks, calm "big sister" voice. Theories 3 and 4 use science (the credibility slides). Theories 1, 2 and 5 are mindset. Theory 5 is the emotional closer.

## Carousel A: weight loss mindset

**Slide 1: Hook**
> 5 viral theories
> that will
> ACTUALLY
> change your body
>
> (from a lifelong
> yo-yo dieter)

**Slide 2**
> **1. the food noise theory.**
>
> most people who "can't stop snacking" don't lack willpower. they're dealing with food noise, the constant chatter about what to eat next.
>
> when it finally goes quiet, you realize you were never lazy. you were just exhausted from fighting your own head all day.

**Slide 3**
> **2. the bad weigh-in theory.**
>
> the scale doesn't move in a straight line. water, salt, sleep and your cycle can swing it 2–5 lbs overnight.
>
> judge your progress by the month, not the morning. the people who win are just the ones who didn't quit on a bad weigh-in.

**Slide 4: product slide**
> **3. the 20 minute theory.**
>
> it takes your gut about 20 minutes to tell your brain you're full. one of the hormones carrying that message is called GLP-1.
>
> for a lot of us that signal is quieter than it should be. i like using [brand] to help my body finally get the message.

**Slide 5**
> **4. the protein first theory.**
>
> eat the protein on your plate before anything else. it keeps you fuller longer and slows how fast sugar hits your blood.
>
> fewer crashes, fewer cravings, and you keep your muscle while the weight comes off. it matters even more when your appetite is smaller.

**Slide 6: closer**
> **5. the old jeans theory.**
>
> keep one pair of jeans from your heaviest season. not to punish yourself, but as proof.
>
> one day they'll slide right off and you'll realize the biggest change wasn't your body. it was finally believing you were worth the effort. you always were.

## Carousel B: self-improvement / habits

**Slide 1: Hook**
> 5 viral theories
> that will
> ACTUALLY
> change your life
>
> (from a former
> all-or-nothing girl)

**Slide 2**
> **1. the never twice theory.**
>
> never skip your routine two days in a row. one missed day is just life. two missed days is the start of a new habit.
>
> you don't need a perfect streak to change. you just need to keep coming back. that's the whole secret.

**Slide 3**
> **2. the empty gym theory.**
>
> you're scared to start because you think everyone at the gym is watching you. they're not. they're counting reps and checking their own form.
>
> the only person who remembers your first awkward workout is you. go anyway.

**Slide 4: product slide**
> **3. the set point theory.**
>
> after you diet, your body raises your hunger hormones to pull you back to the weight it's used to.
>
> that's why it creeps back even when you do everything right. i like using [brand] so my body stops fighting me every step of the way.

**Slide 5**
> **4. the 10 minute walk theory.**
>
> a 10 minute walk right after you eat can noticeably lower your blood sugar spike.
>
> a smaller spike means a smaller crash, fewer cravings and steadier energy all afternoon. it's the easiest habit you'll ever stack.

**Slide 6: closer**
> **5. the mirror theory.**
>
> the way you talk to yourself in the mirror becomes the way you treat your body.
>
> you'll never hate yourself into a body you love. talk to yourself like someone you're proud of. one day you will be.

## Carousel C: glow-up / consistency

**Slide 1: Hook**
> 5 viral theories
> that will
> ACTUALLY
> start your glow up
>
> (from a recovering
> quick-fix addict)

**Slide 2**
> **1. the voting theory.**
>
> every choice you make today is a vote for who you're becoming. the water, the walk, the early night.
>
> you don't have to win every vote. you just need the majority. she's counting on you.

**Slide 3**
> **2. the chapter 20 theory.**
>
> you're comparing your chapter 1 to someone else's chapter 20. her "after" photo took years you never saw.
>
> run your own race at your own pace. the only before and after that matters is yours.

**Slide 4: product slide**
> **3. the bad sleep theory.**
>
> studies show short sleep raises ghrelin, your hunger hormone, and has you craving sugar all the next day.
>
> so half your cravings aren't about discipline, they're chemistry. i like using [brand] to keep my appetite steady so one bad night doesn't wreck my week.

**Slide 5**
> **4. the 3-2-1 theory.**
>
> stop eating 3 hours before bed, stop working 2 hours before, and put your phone down 1 hour before.
>
> your body does its deepest repair while you sleep. give it an empty stomach and a quiet mind and you'll wake up less puffy and more rested.

**Slide 6: closer**
> **5. the marble jar theory.**
>
> picture every healthy choice as a marble in a jar. one salad doesn't fill it. one cookie doesn't empty it.
>
> it's never one moment that changes you. it's the jar slowly filling while you're not looking. keep dropping marbles.
