## 6. ENGAGEMENT: text carousels vs the rest

Like counts are hidden on almost all posts (API returns a placeholder), so comparison uses comment counts.

- Carousels WITH text slides (n=10): comments 3, 5, 10, 21, 21, 24, 33, 44, 45, 70. Median 22.5, mean 27.6.
- Carousels WITHOUT (n=29): median 26, mean 29.9.

**Read: parity.** Text slides neither boost nor hurt a dump. But the split inside the text group matters:
- The two carousels that are ENTIRELY text-driven are her two weakest posts in the whole corpus (250922 manifesto: 5 comments; 260412 bubble joke: 3 comments).
- Carousels with a text card EMBEDDED in a normal photo dump (n=8): median 28.5 comments, at or slightly above her no-text median. The 0716 birthday dump with three cards did 70 comments, well above median.

**Conclusion: an embedded card costs nothing and closes the story; a text-led carousel dies.** The photos carry reach, the card carries meaning.

## 7. IMPLICATIONS for the buried confession card

Our plan: luxury photo-dump carousels where one mid-carousel text card carries a quiet appetite confession. Verdict on nativeness: **supported, with placement guidance.**

### Placement
- Burying a text card inside a photo dump is authentically in her grammar (250926 is the exact template: hot selfie, sincerity card, hot selfie).
- BUT her modal slot is the CLOSER (7 of 10). Two native options for us:
  1. **Mid-dump (position 2 or 3 of 5, or around 60% depth in longer dumps)** for a true "buried" feel, per 250926 and the 0716 meme slot.
  2. **Last slide** for maximum nativeness, per her dominant pattern. A confession-as-closer reads as the emotional thesis of the dump, which is exactly the job our confession does.
- Never slide 1. Slide 1 is always the strongest photo of the character.

### What the card should LOOK like
- **Default: white minimal card.** Small black text, sentence case or lowercase, text block mid-frame or lower third, 60 to 80% whitespace, NO logo, NO watermark, NO hashtag, no headline styling.
- **Upgrade move: solid pastel card color-picked from the dump's palette** (her mint-card trick: card matches the swimsuit in the surrounding slides). This is a strong art-direction signal that the card belongs to this dump.
- Font: clean sans (SF/Helvetica weight) for conversational confessions; small Times-style serif for the more solemn "grateful" register. Reserve bold condensed meme type for jokes only.
- Alternative format for variety: one white serif line overlaid on a no-face luxury photo from the same shoot (sunset from the balcony, the dinner table, the pool). Per 250922, keep it to ONE line, centered, subtle shadow.
- Imperfection is native: lowercase, a casual contraction, even a slightly loose grammar beat. A typographically perfect, branded-looking card would be the tell.

### What the card should SOUND like
- First person, declarative, short. Two sentences max, or the "stacked short lines" cadence:
  - Her gratitude cadence: "Grateful. / Thankful. / Forever blessed."
  - Her notes cadence: "Girl dates are my favorite." then one supporting line.
- The confession should be quiet and matter-of-fact, never explanatory or salesy. Pattern-matched drafts (following the no-em-dash, no-ampersand rule):
  - "Not hungry like I used to be. Not mad about it."
  - "Honestly forgot to finish my plate again. New era."
  - "Quiet flex: I just do not think about food all day anymore."
  - Closer register: "Grateful. / Lighter. / Not chasing cravings anymore."
- Echo the caption. Her cards complete or repeat the caption's thought. Write the caption and the card as one system: the caption plays it casual, the card says the quiet part.
- No product names, no mechanism talk, no CTA on the card. Her cards never sell. The confession earns comments precisely because it withholds.

### The three strongest pattern findings
1. **The text card is a closer by default.** 7 of 10 standalone cards are the final slide, functioning as the emotional thesis of the dump. Mid-dump burial exists (and is precedented for sincerity cards between selfies) but the last-slide confession is her most native slot.
2. **Embedded cards perform at parity, text-led carousels die.** Photos do the reach work; a single card adds meaning at zero engagement cost. Never let the confession become the carousel.
3. **Cards are art-directed as camera-roll artifacts, not graphics.** Whitespace-heavy white cards, palette-matched pastels, screenshot ghosting, typos left in, one watermark in 193 slides. Nativeness comes from looking saved, not designed.
