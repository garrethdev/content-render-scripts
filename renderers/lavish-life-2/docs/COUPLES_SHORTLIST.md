# Couples Shortlist — Black women creators with couple-luxury grids

Search date: 2026-07-27. Goal: aesthetic reference for COUPLE luxury content — brunch/dinner dates,
travel with a boyfriend/husband, his presence in frame (hand, shoulder, across-the-table, walking together).
Vibe: "she's dated well and lives soft" — warm, relational, aspirational-but-attainable luxury.
Excluded: solo fine-art/editorial grids, wedding-content accounts, family-with-kids accounts, mega-celebrities.
Target: 20k-400k followers, photo/carousel-leaning, US-based, couple-POV recurring (not one-off).

Data: ScrapeCreators GET /v1/instagram/profile (12 most recent posts each). Samples in
`muse-search/couples/<handle>/`. Visual compare: `muse-search/couples_shortlist.html`.

Honest note on the pool: this niche skews hard to either skit/relatable Reels couples or
family-vlog accounts. The five below are the best verified matches out of 30 profile checks;
#1-#2 are the true register matches, #3-#5 carry trade-offs listed per entry.

Ranked by fit. The user picks — this is input, not a decision.

## Method + spend
- Discovery: Firecrawl search (listicles, Travel Noire, Feedspot, IG SEO captions, Lemon8), aggregator caption mining, and free owner-identification of candidate posts via Googlebot-UA fetch of IG post pages (og:title + username in HTML)
- Free profile pre-screen trick: Googlebot-UA fetch of instagram.com/<handle> exposes follower count + bio in og:description — used to avoid wasting credits on under-range accounts
- Verification: ScrapeCreators GET /v1/instagram/profile — 30 calls (incl. 3 unresolvable handles). Budget was 30.
- Images: pulled free from IG CDN display_url with browser User-Agent
