## 3. Cheat Meal Rules (expert reframe)
1. I never tell women to skip the cheat meal. I tell them this instead.
2. I don't ban wine. I move it. That one change fixes more than you think.

## 4. The Jealous Friend (quote answered, mystery kept)

2. "How are you never hungry anymore?" I get asked daily. Nobody ever guesses the reason.

## 5. The Quiet Glow-Up (warm validation)

2. I can tell who's going to keep it off. It's never who you'd expect.

## 6. The Backfire (your own "healthy" habit, no villains)

2. I can spot the "healthy" breakfast that's keeping you hungry all day.
3. Half the swaps you made this year are backfiring. I can name them on sight.
